A GUIDE TO DERMAL FILLERS

Dermal fillers are a popular, non-invasive treatment designed to restore volume, smooth wrinkles, and enhance facial features. Most commonly used in areas like the lips, cheeks, and nasolabial folds, fillers work by plumping the skin and improving overall contour. The most common types include hyaluronic acid (HA) fillers, which hydrate and add volume, and calcium hydroxylapatite, which stimulates collagen production for longer-lasting results. The procedure is quick, usually taking 15 to 30 minutes, with minimal downtime and results that can last from 6 months to 2 years, depending on the type used.

When considering dermal fillers, it’s important to consult a qualified professional to ensure safe, natural-looking results. Though generally safe, potential risks include bruising, swelling, or asymmetry. Aftercare is straightforward, but it’s recommended to avoid intense physical activity for the first 24–48 hours. Dermal fillers offer a subtle, non-surgical way to enhance your appearance and restore youthful volume.
