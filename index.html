Our Philosophy · Positive Ageing

Your skin tells the story of a life lived.

Every smile, every challenge, every milestone. We believe in a long-term, personalized approach to medical aesthetics — not quick fixes, but care that evolves as you do.

"Taking care of your appearance shouldn't need justification."

All treatments are performed or supervised by doctors, bringing together clinical safety and deep facial-anatomy expertise. An informed patient is an empowered patient — and our boutique clinic is built around comfort and discretion.

Founders Dr. Zhai & Dr. Van Dewark started Sparrow to close a gap: patients shouldn't have to choose between clinical expertise in a sterile office and a spa experience without medical depth. They wanted both — a Kitsilano space designed to feel personal, inclusive, and empowering.

Meet the Team

A team of physicians, nurses and specialists — every treatment performed or supervised by doctors with expertise in facial anatomy and safety.

Dr. Kenneth Van Dewark

Physician · Medical Aesthetics Specialist

U of Toronto medical school; Fellow of the Royal College of Physicians of Canada; UBC professor teaching medical students and residents. Expert in facial injectables, dermal fillers, biostimulators (Sculptra), neuromodulators, and ultrasound-guided treatments.

Dr. Jacqueline Zhai

Physician · Medical Aesthetics

BSc McGill, MD U of Toronto, postgraduate McMaster; UBC clinical instructor; member of CAAM, CLASS and ASLMS. Combines scientific precision with an artistic approach.

Lorraine Kennedy

Senior Nurse Injector & Trainer

20+ years in medical aesthetics (Botox, fillers, biostimulants, PRP, body). Trains physicians and nurses globally through Merz Aesthetics and speaks at CSAN national conferences.

Dr. Tessa Stark

Licensed Naturopathic Doctor

BSc Physiology & Pharmacology, ND; specializes in aging optimization and regenerative health. Offers Botox, fillers, Sculptra, PRP and body contouring with a natural, conservative approach.

Betty Lum

Sclerotherapy Nurse

35+ years nursing across vascular procedures and interventional radiology, including the BC Children's Hospital Special Procedures team. 10 years treating varicose veins with a patient-centered approach.
