Our Philosophy — Positive Ageing

Care that evolves as you do.

"Your skin tells the story of a life lived — every smile, every challenge, every milestone."

We believe in a long-term, personalized approach to medical aesthetics — not quick fixes. Every treatment is performed or supervised by doctors, grounded in safety and deep facial-anatomy expertise.

An informed patient is an empowered patient. Sparrow is a boutique clinic that combines genuine comfort and discretion with real medical depth.

"Taking care of your appearance shouldn't need justification."

Founders Dr. Zhai & Dr. Van Dewark started Sparrow to close a gap: patients shouldn't have to choose between clinical expertise in a sterile office and a spa experience without medical depth — they wanted both. Our Kitsilano space is designed to feel personal, inclusive, and empowering.
